# Chapter 9 proof review

Reviewed every statement and proof in 9A–9D and the sixteen optional exercises. Symmetric bilinear diagonalization uses congruence and algebraic induction over both fields; only the real inner-product version asserts an orthonormal basis. Permutation parity is established by inversions and arbitrary swaps before it is used for alternating forms. The normalized coordinate alternating form is constructed and proved alternating without a determinant argument. Zero-arity forms ensure determinant one on the zero space. Determinant multiplicativity follows from pullbacks, transpose from inverse permutation signs, and the real Cayley–Hamilton theorem from the same real matrix acting on complex coordinates. Added explicit triangularization dependencies, standardized sign notation, separated the dimension-one characteristic-polynomial display, and expanded the orthogonal-edge basis argument. The proper-subspace measure-zero lemma and singular volume theorem received an independent second review; covers, measurability, infinite outer measure, and dimension-one cases passed. Tensor products are bilinear functionals on the dual factors. Their universal properties are constructed from unique basis coordinates, and inner-product existence checks every axiom before using uniqueness. All multi-factor results have full proofs rather than omitted analogies. All zero-factor cases are handled.

The exercise set received an independent statement/proof/dependency review. Added explicit inverses for two basis maps, verified linearity of the rank-one perturbation, added the missing primitive dependencies, and corrected the abstract-space volume application to use the earlier general singular-value volume theorem. Strengthened the two-pure-tensor exercise to an if-and-only-if characterization and introduced the hypothetical factor vectors explicitly. No exercise depends on another exercise. No unresolved mathematical issues were identified in these reviews.
